# Warm-Up

- Leg swings (front-back, then side-side) — 10 swings each position & leg
- Walking high knees — 20 yards
- Walking butt kicks — 20 yards
- Calf scoops — 20 yards
- Walking lunges w/ torso twist towards front leg — 8 each side
- Ankle bounces — 20 reps

# Core Workout

**Goal:** 4-5 miles @ 6:15/mi pace

# Cool-Down

- Reclined quad stretch — 45s / side
- Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 45s / side
- Butterfly — 30s / side
- One-legged V-Sit stretch — 30s / side
- Seated straight leg stretch — 30s
- Figure-4 glute stretch — 45s / side
- Calf stretch against wall — 30s each position & side
	- Bent-knee (soleus)
	- Straight-knee (gastroc)
- 90/90 hip rotations — 2-3 slow reps / side
- Thoracic extension over a foam roller — 6-8 reps
- Doorway pec stretch — 30s
