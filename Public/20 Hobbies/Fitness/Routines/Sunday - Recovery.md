Total time: ~57-63 mins

# Warm-Up

- Could be walking, easy cycling, or swimming (not running)

Total time: 5-8 mins

# Core Workout

## Foam Rolling / Myofascial Release

- Calves — 90s/leg
- Quads — 90s/leg
- IT band — 60s/leg (light pressure)
- Glutes/piriformis — 90s/side
- Upper back/lats — 2 mins
- Forearms — 60s/arm

Total time: 15 mins

## Deep Static Stretching

- Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 60-90s/side
- Hamstring stretch — 60-90s/side
- Pancake/straddle hold — 2 mins
- Figure-4 glute stretch — 60s/side
- Pigeon pose — 90s/side
- Doorway pec stretch — 60s
- Overhead lat stretch — 60s/side

Total time: 18-20 mins

## Joint Mobility / CARs Circuit

- Shoulder CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per arm
- HIP CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per leg
- Ankle CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per foot
- Wrist CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per hand
- Cat-cow into thread-the-needle — 8 reps then 6 rotations, per side

Total time: 12-15 mins

# Cool-Down

- Box breathing (4s in, 4s hold, 6s out) — 5 mins total
	- Laying down, legs elevated against wall

Total time: 5 mins
