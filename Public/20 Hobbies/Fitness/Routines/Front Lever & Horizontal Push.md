# Warm-Up

- General pulse-raiser
	1. [[Jump Rope|Jump rope]] — 60s
	2. [[Cossack Squat|Cossack squats]] — 10 reps
	3. [[Leg Swing|Standing leg swings]] (front-back) — 10/leg
- Joint prep
	1. [[Band Pull-Apart|Band pull aparts]] — 20 reps
	2. [[Wrist Prep|Wrist prep]] (rocks, circles) — 90s
	3. [[Scapular Push-up|Scapular pushups]] — 15 reps
	4. [[Scapular Pull-up|Scapular pull-ups]] — 10 reps
	5. [[Good Morning Reach|Good morning reach]] — 10 reps

Rest: None  
Total time: ~10 mins

# Core Workout

## Front Lever (FL) Progression

1. ==[[Hollow Hold|Hollow body hold]] — 3x45s, no lower back arch==
2. [[Hanging Tuck Hold|Hanging tuck hold]] — 20s, flat back no swing
3. [[Tuck Front Lever|Hanging Tuck FL]] — 20s, flat back
4. [[One-Leg Front Lever|One-leg FL]] — 15s / side
5. [[Straddle Front Lever|Straddle FL]] — 12s
6. [[Front Lever|Full front lever]] — 10s

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~12 mins

## One Arm Push-Up (OAP) Progression

1. ==[[Incline Push-up|Incline push-ups]] — 3x20==
2. [[Push-up|Standard push-ups]] — 3x25
3. [[Diamond Push-up|Diamond pushups]] — 3x15
4. [[Decline Push-up|Decline pushups]] — 3x15
5. [[Archer Push-up|Archer pushups]] — 3x8/side
6. [[Assisted One-Arm Push-up|Assisted OAP]] — 5 reps/side
7. [[One-Arm Push-up|One Arm Push-Up]]

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3x5-10  
Total time: ~10 mins

## Nordic Hamstring Progression

1. [[Single-Leg Glute Bridge|Single-leg glute bridge]] — 3x10/leg
2. [[Hamstring Slide|Hamstring slides]] — 3x12
3. ==[[Assisted Nordic Curl|Assisted Nordic]] — 3x8==
4. [[Eccentric Nordic Curl|Eccentric Nordic]] — 3x6
5. [[Nordic Curl|Strict Nordic Curl]]

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3x3-8  
Total time: ~9 mins

## Ring Dip Progression

1. [[Bench Dip|Bench dip]] — 3x20, full lockout
2. ==[[Parallel Bar Dip|Parallel bar dips]] — 3x12==
3. [[Deep Dip|Deep dips]] — 3x10, full ROM
4. [[Ring Dip|Ring dips]] — 3x8, minimal ring wobble
5. [[Weighted Ring Dip|Weighted ring dips]]

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3x5-8  
Total time: ~8 mins

## L-Sit Progression

1. ==[[Tuck L-Sit Hold|Tuck L-sit hold]] — 3x30s, hips fully off ground==
2. [[One-Leg L-Sit Hold|One-leg L-sit]] — 3x30s/leg
3. [[L-Sit Hold|Full L-sit]] — 3x20s
4. [[V-Sit Hold|V-sit hold]]

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~8 mins
