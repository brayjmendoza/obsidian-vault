# Progressions

## Front Lever (FL) Progression

Total time: ~12 min

1. ==[[Hollow Hold|Hollow body hold]] — Sets: 3, Time: 45s, Rest: 2 min, Cue: no lower back arch==
2. [[Hanging Tuck Hold|Hanging tuck hold]] — Sets: 3, Time: 20s, Rest: 2 min, Cue: flat back, no swing
3. [[Tuck Front Lever|Hanging tuck FL]] — Sets: 3, Time: 20s, Rest: 2 min, Cue: flat back
4. [[One-Leg Front Lever|One-leg FL]] — Sets: 3, Time: 15s/side, Rest: 2 min
5. [[Straddle Front Lever|Straddle FL]] — Sets: 3, Time: 12s, Rest: 2 min
6. [[Front Lever|Full front lever]] — Sets: 3, Time: 10s, Rest: 2 min

## One-Arm Push-Up (OAP) Progression

Guideline: 3x5-10

1. ==[[Incline Push-up|Incline push-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 20, Rest: 2-3 min==

3. [[Diamond Push-up|Diamond push-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 15, Rest: 2-3 min
4. [[Decline Push-up|Decline push-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 15, Rest: 2-3 min
5. [[Archer Push-up|Archer push-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 8/side, Rest: 2-3 min
6. [[Assisted One-Arm Push-up|Assisted OAP]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 5/side, Rest: 2-3 min
7. [[One-Arm Push-up|One-arm push-up]]

## Nordic Hamstring Progression

Guideline: 3x3-8

1. [[Single-Leg Glute Bridge|Single-leg glute bridge]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 10/leg, Rest: 2-3 min

4. [[Eccentric Nordic Curl|Eccentric Nordic curls]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 6, Rest: 2-3 min

## Ring Dip Progression

Guideline: 3x5-8  
Total time: ~8 min

1. [[Bench Dip|Bench dips]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 20, Rest: 2-3 min, Cue: full lockout

4. [[Ring Dip|Ring dips]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 8, Rest: 2-3 min, Cue: minimal ring wobble
