# Warm-Up

- General pulse-raiser
	1. [[Jump Rope|Jump rope]] — 1 min
- Dynamic hip flow
	1. [[Leg Swing|Leg swings]] (front-back, then lateral) — 8 each leg, each direction
	2. [[Cossack Squat|Cossack squats]] — 8 reps
	3. [[Walking Lunge with Knee Drive|Walking lunges w/ knee drive]] — 6/leg
- Joint prep
	1. [[Wrist Prep|Wrist prep]] (rocks, circles, wall wrist stretch) — 90s
	2. [[Scapular Pull-up|Scapular pull-ups]] — 10 reps
	3. [[Scapular Push-up|Scapular push-ups]] — 10 reps
	4. [[Band Shoulder External Rotation|Band shoulder external rotation]] — 15 reps
	5. [[Ankle Bounce|Ankle bounces]] — 15-20 reps

Rest: None  
Total time: ~10-12 mins

# Core Workout

## Explosive Legs

1. [[Jump Squat|Jump squats]] — 3x10, silent landings & no knee cave
2. [[Broad Jump|Broad jumps]] — 3x6, consistent distance & stable landing
3. [[Box Jump|Box jumps]] — 3x6, full hip extension at top, controlled step-down
4. ==[[Single-Leg Hop|Single-leg hops]] — 3x8/leg, stable landing==
5. [[Lateral Bound|Lateral bounds]] — 3x6/leg, stick the landing
6. [[Single-Leg Box Jump|Single-leg box jumps]] or [[Single-Leg Depth Jump|single-leg depth jumps]]

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3 (get as close to benchmark with good form, stop once form slips)  
Total time: ~7-9 mins  
**Note:** Keep volume low due to running

## Muscle-Up Progression

1. ==[[Assisted Pull-up|Assisted pull-ups]] — 3x10==
2. [[Pull-up|Pull-ups]] — 3x10
3. [[Chest-to-Bar Pull-up|Chest-to-bar pull-ups]] — 3x8
4. [[Explosive Pull-up|Explosive pull-ups]], navel height — 3x5, clearly above navel line
5. [[Band-Assisted Muscle-up|Band-assisted muscle-ups]] — 3x5, lightest band, no kipping/swinging
6. [[Negative Muscle-up|Negative muscle-up]] — 3x5, 6s descent no drop
7. [[Muscle-up|Strict muscle-up]] — 3x5
8. [[Slow Muscle-up|Slow controlled muscle-up]]

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3
Total time: ~15 mins

## Planche Hold Progression

1. [[Straight-Arm Plank|Straight arm plank]] — 3x45s, no hip sag
2. ==[[Planche Lean|Planche lean]] — 3x30s==
3. [[Frog Stand|Frog stand]] — 30s
4. [[Tuck Planche|Tuck planche]] — 15s
5. [[Advanced Tuck Planche|Advanced tuck planche]] — 15s, hips level & no pike
6. [[Straddle Planche|Straddle planche]] — 12s
7. [[Planche|Full Planche]]

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~8 mins

## Planche Push-Up Progression

1. ==[[Push-up|Push-ups]] — 3x20==
2. [[Incline Pseudo Planche Push-up|Incline pseudo planche push-ups]] — 3x12
3. [[Pseudo Planche Push-up|Pseudo planche pushups]] — 3x15
4. [[Planche Lean Push-up|Lean pushups]] — 3x12 reps
5. [[Tuck Planche Push-up|Tuck planche pushups]] — 3x8 reps, controlled tempo
6. [[Advanced Tuck Planche Push-up|Advanced tuck pushups]] — 3x6 reps
7. [[Straddle Planche Push-up|Straddle planche pushups]]

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3
Total time: ~12 mins

## Front Lever Rows

1. [[Inverted Row|Inverted rows]] — 3x12
2. ==[[Feet-Elevated Inverted Row|Feet elevated inverted rows]] — 3x10==
3. [[Tuck Front Lever Row|Tuck front lever rows]] — 3x8, flat back & controlled pull to chest
4. [[Advanced Tuck Front Lever Row|Advanced tuck rows]] — 3x8
5. [[Front Lever Row|Full front lever rows]]

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3
Total time: ~8-9 mins

# Cool-Down

1. [[Jefferson Curl|Bodyweight Jefferson curls]] — 8 slow tempo (3-4s down, 3-4s up)
2. [[Hamstring Stretch|Basic hamstring stretch]] — 45s/leg
3. [[Pancake Hold|Pancake stretch]] — 90s, sinking deeper
4. [[Pike Compression Hold|Pike compression holds]] — 3x20-30s (knees lifted, stop before form breaks)
