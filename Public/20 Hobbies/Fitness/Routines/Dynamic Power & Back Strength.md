# Progressions

## Explosive Legs

Get as close to the benchmark as good form allows, and stop once form slips. Keep volume low due to running.  
Total time: ~7-9 min

1. [[Jump Squat|Jump squats]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 10, Rest: 2 min, Cue: silent landings and no knee cave
2. [[Broad Jump|Broad jumps]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 6, Rest: 2 min, Cue: consistent distance and stable landing
3. [[Box Jump|Box jumps]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 6, Rest: 2 min, Cue: full hip extension at top, controlled step-down
4. ==[[Single-Leg Hop|Single-leg hops]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 8/leg, Rest: 2 min, Cue: stable landing==
5. [[Lateral Bound|Lateral bounds]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 6/leg, Rest: 2 min, Cue: stick the landing
6. [[Single-Leg Box Jump|Single-leg box jumps]] or [[Single-Leg Depth Jump|single-leg depth jumps]]

## Muscle-Up Progression

Total time: ~15 min

1. ==[[Assisted Pull-up|Assisted pull-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 10, Rest: 3 min==
2. [[Pull-up|Pull-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 10, Rest: 3 min
3. [[Chest-to-Bar Pull-up|Chest-to-bar pull-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 8, Rest: 3 min
4. [[Explosive Pull-up|Explosive pull-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 5, Rest: 3 min, Cue: navel height, clearly above navel line
5. [[Band-Assisted Muscle-up|Band-assisted muscle-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 5, Assistance: lightest band, Rest: 3 min, Cue: no kipping or swinging
6. [[Negative Muscle-up|Negative muscle-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 5, Rest: 3 min, Tempo: 6s descent, no drop
7. [[Muscle-up|Strict muscle-ups]] — Sets: 3, Reps: 5, Rest: 3 min
8. [[Slow Muscle-up|Slow controlled muscle-up]]
