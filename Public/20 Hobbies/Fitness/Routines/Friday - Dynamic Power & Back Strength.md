# Warm Up

- General pulse-raiser
	1. Jump rope — 1 min
- Dynamic hip flow
	1. Leg swings (front-back, then lateral) — 8 each leg, each direction
	2. Cossack squats — 8 reps
	3. Walking lunges w/ knee drive — 6/leg
- Joint prep
	1. Wrist prep (rocks, circles, wall wrist stretch) — 90s
	2. Scapular pull-ups — 10 reps
	3. Scapular push-ups — 10 reps
	4. Band shoulder external rotation — 15 reps
	5. Ankle bounces — 15-20 reps

Rest: None  
Total time: ~10-12 mins

# Explosive Legs

1. Jump squats (3x10, silent landings & no knee cave)
2. Broad jumps (3x6, consistent distance & stable landing)
3. Box jumps (3x6, full hip extension at top, controlled step-down)
4. ==Single-leg hops (3x8/leg, stable landing)==
5. Lateral bounds (3x6/leg, stick the landing)
6. Single-leg box jumps or single-leg depth jumps

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3 (get as close to benchmark with good form, stop once form slips)  
Total time: ~7-9 mins  
**Note:** Keep volume low due to running

# Muscle-Up Progression

1. ==Assisted pull-ups (3x10)==
2. Pull-ups (3x10)
3. Chest-to-bar pull-ups (3x8)
4. Explosive pull-ups, navel height (3x5, clearly above navel line)
5. Band-assisted muscle-ups (3x5, lightest band, no kipping/swinging)
6. Negative muscle-up (3x5, 6s descent no drop)
7. Strict muscle-up (3x5)
8. Slow controlled muscle-up

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3x5  
Total time: ~15 mins

# Planche Hold Progression

1. Straight arm plank (3x45s, no hip sag)
2. ==Planche lean (3x30s)==
3. Frog stand (30s)
4. Tuck planche (15s)
5. Advanced tuck planche (15s, hips level & no pike)
6. Straddle planche (12s)
7. Full Planche

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~8 mins

# Planche Push-Up Progression

1. ==Push-ups (3x20)==
2. Incline pseudo planche push-ups (3x12)
3. Pseudo planche pushups (3x15)
4. Lean pushups (3x12 reps)
5. Tuck planche pushups (3x8 reps, controlled tempo)
6. Advanced tuck pushups (3x6 reps)
7. Straddle planche pushups

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3x5-10  
Total time: ~12 mins

# Front Lever Rows

1. Inverted rows (3x12)
2. ==Feet elevated inverted rows (3x10)==
3. Tuck front lever rows (3x8, flat back & controlled pull to chest)
4. Advanced tuck rows (3x8)
5. Full front lever rows

Rest: 3 mins  
Sets: 3x5-8  
Total time: ~8-9 mins

# Mobility/Compression Finisher

1. Bodyweight Jefferson curls — 8 slow tempo (3-4s down, 3-4s up)
2. Basic hamstring stretch — 45s/leg
3. Pancake stretch — 90s, sinking deeper
4. Pike compression holds — 3x20-30s (knees lifted, stop before form breaks)
