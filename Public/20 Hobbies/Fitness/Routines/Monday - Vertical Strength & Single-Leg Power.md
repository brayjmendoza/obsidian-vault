# Warm-Up

- General pulse-raiser
	1. [[Jump Rope|Jump rope]] — 60s
	2. [[Arm Circle|Arm circles]] (forward/backward) — 15s each direction
	3. [[Leg Swing|Leg swings]] (front-back, then lateral) — 10 each leg, each direction
- Joint prep
	1. [[Wrist Rock|Wrist rocks]] (both directions) — 2x15
	2. [[Knee-to-Wall Ankle Dorsiflexion Drill|Knee-to-wall ankle dorsiflexion drill]] — 2x8/side
	3. [[Scapular Pull-up|Scapular pull-ups]] — 2x10
	4. [[Deep Squat Hold with Thoracic Rotation|Deep squat hold w/ thoracic rotation]] — 6 reaches/side
	5. [[Hollow Hold|Hollow hold]] — 2x20s

Rest: None  
Total time: ~10 mins

# Core Workout

## Handstand Push-Up (HSPU) Progression

1. ==[[Push-up|Push-ups]] (3x20)==
2. [[Pike Push-up|Pike push-ups]] (3x12)
3. [[Feet-Elevated Pike Push-up|Feet-elevated pike push-ups]] (3x10)
4. [[Chest-to-Wall Handstand Hold|Chest-to-wall Wall handstand hold]] (3x45s solid)
5. [[Wall Negative Handstand Push-up|Wall negative HSPU]] (3x3 w/ 5-8s descent)
6. [[Partial Wall Handstand Push-up|Wall HSPU partial ROM]] (4x5)
7. [[Wall Handstand Push-up|Full wall HSPU]] (4x8)
8. [[Deficit Wall Handstand Push-up|Deficit wall HSPU]] (4x6)
9. [[Freestanding Handstand Push-up|Freestanding HSPU]]

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~16 mins

## One Arm Pull-Up Progression

1. ==[[Assisted Pull-up|Assisted pull-ups]] (3x10 w/ lightest assistance)==
2. [[Negative Pull-up|Negative pull-ups]] (5 reps w/ 8s controlled descent)
3. [[Pull-up|Pull-ups]] (3x10 strict)
4. [[Chest-to-Bar Pull-up|Chest-to-bar pull-ups]] (3x8)
5. [[Archer Pull-up|Archer pull-ups]] (3x5/side with straight extended arm)
6. [[Assisted One-Arm Pull-up|Assisted one-arm pull-ups]] (5 clean reps per side)
7. [[One-Arm Negative Pull-up|One-arm negatives]] (5-8s descent, 5 reps/side)
8. [[One-Arm Pull-up|One arm pull-up]]

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~10 mins

## Hanging Compression

1. [[Hanging Knee Raise|Hanging knee raises]] — 3x12 (benchmark: 3x15, no swing)
2. ==[[Hanging Leg Raise|Hanging leg raises]] — 3x10 (3x12, no kip)==
3. [[Toes-to-Bar|Toes-to-bar]] — 3x8 (3x10, controlled descent)
4. [[Tuck L-Sit Hold|L-sit tuck hold]] — 3x20-30s (3x30s, hips at/above parallel)
5. [[L-Sit Hold|L-sit full hold]] — 3x10-15s (3x20s)
6. [[L-Sit Leg Raise|L-sit leg raises]] — 3x8 (3x10)
7. [[V-up|V-ups]] — 3x12-15 (3x15, toes to bar height)
8. [[Straddle L-Sit Hold|Straddle L-sit]] / [[V-Sit Hold|V-sit hold]]

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~10 minutes

## Single Leg Squat Progression

1. [[Bodyweight Squat|Bodyweight squats]] (3x20)
2. [[Split Squat|Split squats]] (3x12 / leg)
3. [[Bulgarian Split Squat|Bulgarian split squats]] (3x10 / leg)
4. [[Step-down|Step-downs]], slow eccentric (3x8/leg w/ 5s descent)
5. [[Box Pistol Squat|Box pistol squats]] (3x8/leg)
6. ==[[Assisted Pistol Squat|Assisted pistol squats]] (3x6/leg)==
7. [[Pistol Squat|Full pistol squats]] (3x8)
8. [[Deficit Pistol Squat|Deficit pistol squats]] (3x6)
9. [[Shrimp Squat|Shrimp squat]] / [[Weighted Pistol Squat|weighted pistol]]

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~10-12 mins

# Cool-Down

1. [[Pancake Hold|Pancake hold]] — 2x60s, sink deeper each round
2. [[Overhead Shoulder Stretch|Wall/doorway overhead shoulder stretch]] — 2x45-60s
3. [[Standing Calf Stretch|Standing calf stretch]] — 45s both legs, soleus and gastroc
	- Soleus: bent-knee
	- Gatroc: straight-knee
4. [[Wrist Flexor and Extensor Stretch|Wrist flexor/extensor stretch]] — 30s each direction, both hands

Rest: None  
Total time: 7-8 mins
