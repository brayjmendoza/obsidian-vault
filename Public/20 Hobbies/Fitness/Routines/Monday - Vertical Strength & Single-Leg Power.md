# Warm Up

- General pulse-raiser
	1. Jump rope — 60s
	2. Arm circles (forward/backward) — 15s each direction
	3. Leg swings (front-back, then lateral) — 10 each leg, each direction
- Joint prep
	1. Wrist rocks (both directions) — 2x15
	2. Knee-to-wall ankle dorsiflexion drill — 2x8/side
	3. Scapular pull-ups — 2x10
	4. Deep squat hold w/ thoracic rotation — 6 reaches/side
	5. Hollow hold — 2x20s

Rest: None  
Total time: ~10 mins

# Handstand Push-Up (HSPU) Progression

1. ==Push-ups (3x20)==
2. Pike push-ups (3x12)
3. Feet-elevated pike push-ups (3x10)
4. Chest-to-wall Wall handstand hold (3x45s solid)
5. Wall negative HSPU (3x3 w/ 5-8s descent)
6. Wall HSPU partial ROM (4x5)
7. Full wall HSPU (4x8)
8. Deficit wall HSPU (4x6)
9. Freestanding HSPU

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~16 mins

# One Arm Pull-Up Progression

1. ==Assisted pull-ups (3x10 w/ lightest assistance)==
2. Negative pull-ups (5 reps w/ 8s controlled descent)
3. Pull-ups (3x10 strict)
4. Chest-to-bar pull-ups (3x8)
5. Archer pull-ups (3x5/side with straight extended arm)
6. Assisted one-arm pull-ups (5 clean reps per side)
7. One-arm negatives (5-8s descent, 5 reps/side)
8. One arm pull-up

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~10 mins

# Hanging Compression

1. Hanging knee raises — 3x12 (benchmark: 3x15, no swing)
2. ==Hanging leg raises — 3x10 (3x12, no kip)==
3. Toes-to-bar — 3x8 (3x10, controlled descent)
4. L-sit tuck hold — 3x20-30s (3x30s, hips at/above parallel)
5. L-sit full hold — 3x10-15s (3x20s)
6. L-sit leg raises — 3x8 (3x10)
7. V-ups — 3x12-15 (3x15, toes to bar height)
8. Straddle L-sit / V-sit hold

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~10 minutes

# Single Leg Squat Progression

1. Bodyweight squats (3x20)
2. Split squats (3x12 / leg)
3. Bulgarian split squats (3x10 / leg)
4. Step-downs, slow eccentric (3x8/leg w/ 5s descent)
5. Box pistol squats (3x8/leg)
6. ==Assisted pistol squats (3x6/leg)==
7. Full pistol squats (3x8)
8. Deficit pistol squats (3x6)
9. Shrimp squat / weighted pistol

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~10-12 mins

# Mobility Finisher

1. Pancake hold — 2x60s, sink deeper each round
2. Wall/doorway overhead shoulder stretch — 2x45-60s
3. Standing calf stretch — 45s both legs, soleus and gastroc
	- Soleus: bent-knee
	- Gatroc: straight-knee
4. Wrist flexor/extensor stretch — 30s each direction, both hands

Rest: None  
Total time: 7-8 mins
