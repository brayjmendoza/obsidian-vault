# Warm Up

- General pulse-raiser
	1. Jump rope — 60s
	2. Cossack squats — 10 reps
	3. Standing leg swings (front-back) — 10/leg
- Joint prep
	1. Band pull aparts — 20 reps
	2. Wrist prep (rocks, circles) — 90s
	3. Scapular pushups — 15 reps
	4. Scapular pull-ups — 10 reps
	5. Good morning reach — 10 reps

Rest: None  
Total time: ~10 mins

# Front Lever (FL) Progression

1. ==Hollow body hold (3x45s, no lower back arch)==
2. Hanging tuck hold (20s, flat back no swing)
3. Hanging Tuck FL (20s, flat back)
4. One-leg FL (15s / side)
5. Straddle FL (12s)
6. Full front lever (10s)

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~12 mins

# One Arm Push-Up (OAP) Progression

1. ==Incline push-ups (3x20)==
2. Standard push-ups (3x25)
3. Diamond pushups (3x15)
4. Decline pushups (3x15)
5. Archer pushups (3x8/side)
6. Assisted OAP (5 reps/side)
7. One Arm Push-Up

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3x5-10  
Total time: ~10 mins

# Nordic Hamstring Progression

1. Single-leg glute bridge (3x10/leg)
2. Hamstring slides (3x12)
3. ==Assisted Nordic (3x8)==
4. Eccentric Nordic (3x6)
5. Strict Nordic Curl

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3x3-8  
Total time: ~9 mins

# Ring Dip Progression

1. Bench dip (3x20, full lockout)
2. ==Parallel bar dips (3x12)==
3. Deep dips (3x10, full ROM)
4. Ring dips (3x8, minimal ring wobble)
5. Weighted ring dips

Rest: 2-3 mins  
Sets: 3x5-8  
Total time: ~8 mins

# L-Sit Progression

1. ==Tuck L-sit hold (3x30s, hips fully off ground)==
2. One-leg L-sit (3x30s/leg)
3. Full L-sit (3x20s)
4. V-sit hold

Rest: 2 mins  
Sets: 3  
Total time: ~8 mins

# Mobility Finisher

1. Dead hang — 2x45-60s
	- Alternative: standing overhead reach with side bend
2. Doorway pec stretch — 2x45s
3. Standing hamstring stretch — 45s/leg
4. Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 45s/leg

Rest: None  
Total time: ~8 mins
