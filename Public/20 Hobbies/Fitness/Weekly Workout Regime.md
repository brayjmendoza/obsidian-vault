## Monday: Vertical Strength + Single-Leg Power
- Warm-Up
    - General pulse-raiser
        1. Jump rope — 60s
        2. Arm circles (forward/backward) — 15s each direction
        3. Leg swings (front-back, then lateral) — 10 each leg, each direction
    - Joint prep
        1. Wrist rocks (both directions) — 2x15
        2. Knee-to-wall ankle dorsiflexion drill — 2x8/side
        3. Scapular pull-ups — 2x10
        4. Deep squat hold w/ thoracic rotation — 6 reaches/side
        5. Hollow hold — 2x20s
    
    Rest: None  
    Total time: ~10 mins
    
- Handstand Push-Up (HSPU) Progression
    1. ==Push-ups (3x20)==
    2. Pike push-ups (3x12)
    3. Feet-elevated pike push-ups (3x10)
    4. Chest-to-wall Wall handstand hold (3x45s solid)
    5. Wall negative HSPU (3x3 w/ 5-8s descent)
    6. Wall HSPU partial ROM (4x5)
    7. Full wall HSPU (4x8)
    8. Deficit wall HSPU (4x6)
    9. Freestanding HSPU
    
    Rest: 2-3 mins  
    Sets: 3  
    Total time: ~16 mins
    
- One Arm Pull-Up Progression
    1. ==Assisted pull-ups (3x10 w/ lightest assistance)==
    2. Negative pull-ups (5 reps w/ 8s controlled descent)
    3. Pull-ups (3x10 strict)
    4. Chest-to-bar pull-ups (3x8)
    5. Archer pull-ups (3x5/side with straight extended arm)
    6. Assisted one-arm pull-ups (5 clean reps per side)
    7. One-arm negatives (5-8s descent, 5 reps/side)
    8. One arm pull-up
    
    Rest: 2-3 mins  
    Sets: 3  
    Total time: ~10mins
    
- Hanging Compression
    1. Hanging knee raises — 3x12 (benchmark: 3x15, no swing)
    2. ==Hanging leg raises — 3x10 (3x12, no kip)==
    3. Toes-to-bar — 3x8 (3x10, controlled descent)
    4. L-sit tuck hold — 3x20-30s (3x30s, hips at/above parallel)
    5. L-sit full hold — 3x10-15s (3x20s)
    6. L-sit leg raises — 3x8 (3x10)
    7. V-ups — 3x12-15 (3x15, toes to bar height)
    8. Straddle L-sit / V-sit hold
    
    Rest: 2-3 mins  
    Sets: 3  
    Total time: ~10 minutes
    
- Single Leg Squat Progression
    1. Bodyweight squats (3x20)
    2. Split squats (3x12 / leg)
    3. Bulgarian split squats (3x10 / leg)
    4. Step-downs, slow eccentric (3x8/leg w/ 5s descent)
    5. Box pistol squats (3x8/leg)
    6. ==Assisted pistol squats (3x6/leg)==
    7. Full pistol squats (3x8)
    8. Deficit pistol squats (3x6)
    9. Shrimp squat / weighted pistol
    
    Rest: 2 mins  
    Sets: 3  
    Total time: ~10-12 mins
    
- Mobility Finisher
    1. Pancake hold — 2x60s, sink deeper each round
    2. Wall/doorway overhead shoulder stretch — 2x45-60s
    3. Standing calf stretch — 45s both legs, soleus and gastroc
        - Soleus: bent-knee
        - Gatroc: straight-knee
    4. Wrist flexor/extensor stretch — 30s each direction, both hands
    
    Rest: None  
    Total time: 7-8mins

## Wednesday: Front Lever + Horizontal Push

- Warm-Up
    - General pulse-raiser
        1. Jump rope — 60s
        2. Cossack squats — 10 reps
        3. Standing leg swings (front-back) — 10/leg
    - Joint prep
        1. Band pull aparts — 20 reps
        2. Wrist prep (rocks, circles) — 90s
        3. Scapular pushups — 15 reps
        4. Scapular pull-ups — 10 reps
        5. Good morning reach — 10 reps
    
    Rest: None  
    Total time: ~10 mins
    
- Front Lever (FL) Progression
    1. ==Hollow body hold (3x45s, no lower back arch)==
    2. Hanging tuck hold (20s, flat back no swing)
    3. Hanging Tuck FL (20s, flat back)
    4. One-leg FL (15s / side)
    5. Straddle FL (12s)
    6. Full front lever (10s)
    
    Rest: 2 mins  
    Sets: 3
    Total time: ~12 mins
    
- One Arm Push-Up (OAP) Progression
    1. ==Incline push-ups (3x20)==
    2. Standard push-ups (3x25)
    3. Diamond pushups (3x15)
    4. Decline pushups (3x15)
    5. Archer pushups (3x8/side)
    6. Assisted OAP (5 reps/side)
    7. One Arm Push-Up
    
    Rest: 2-3 mins  
    Sets: 3x5-10  
    Total time: ~10 mins
    
- Nordic Hamstring Progression
    1. Single-leg glute bridge (3x10/leg)
    2. Hamstring slides (3x12)
    3. ==Assisted Nordic (3x8)==
    4. Eccentric Nordic (3x6) 
    5. Strict Nordic Curl
    
    Rest: 2-3 mins  
    Sets: 3x3-8  
    Total time: ~9 mins
    
- Ring Dip Progression
    1. Bench dip (3x20, full lockout)
    2. ==Parallel bar dips (3x12)==
    3. Deep dips (3x10, full ROM)
    4. Ring dips (3x8, minimal ring wobble)
    5. Weighted ring dips
    
    Rest: 2-3 mins  
    Sets: 3x5-8  
    Total time: ~8 mins
    
- L-Sit Progression
    1. ==Tuck L-sit hold (3x30s, hips fully off ground)==
    2. One-leg L-sit (3x30s/leg)
    3. Full L-sit (3x20s)
    4. V-sit hold
    
    Rest: 2 mins  
    Sets: 3  
    Total time: ~8 mins
    
- Mobility finisher
    1. Dead hang — 2x45-60s
        - Alternative: standing overhead reach with side bend
    2. Doorway pec stretch — 2x45s
    3. Standing hamstring stretch — 45s/leg
    4. Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 45s/leg
    
    Rest: None  
    Total time: ~8 mins

## Friday: Dynamic Power + Back Strength

- Warm-Up
    - General pulse-raiser
        1. Jump rope — 1 min
    - Dynamic hip flow
        1. Leg swings (front-back, then lateral) — 8 each leg, each direction
        2. Cossack squats — 8 reps
        3. Walking lunges w/ knee drive — 6/leg
    - Joint prep
        1. Wrist prep (rocks, circles, wall wrist stretch) — 90s
        2. Scapular pull-ups — 10 reps
        3. Scapular push-ups — 10 reps
        4. Band shoulder external rotation — 15 reps
        5. Ankle bounces — 15-20 reps
    
    Rest: None  
    Total time: ~10-12 mins
    
- Explosive Legs
    1. Jump squats (3x10, silent landings & no knee cave)
    2. Broad jumps (3x6, consistent distance & stable landing)
    3. Box jumps (3x6, full hip extension at top, controlled step-down)
    4. ==Single-leg hops (3x8/leg, stable landing)==
    5. Lateral bounds (3x6/leg, stick the landing)
    6. Single-leg box jumps or single-leg depth jumps
    
    Rest: 2 mins  
    Sets: 3 (get as close to benchmark with good form, stop once form slips)  
    Total time: ~7-9 mins  
    **Note:** Keep volume low due to running
    
- Muscle-Up Progression
    1. ==Assisted pull-ups (3x10)==
    2. Pull-ups (3x10)
    3. Chest-to-bar pull-ups (3x8)
    4. Explosive pull-ups, navel height (3x5, clearly above navel line)
    5. Band-assisted muscle-ups (3x5, lightest band, no kipping/swinging)
    6. Negative muscle-up (3x5, 6s descent no drop)
    7. Strict muscle-up (3x5)
    8. Slow controlled muscle-up
    
    Rest: 3 mins  
    Sets: 3x5  
    Total time: ~15 mins
    
- Planche Hold Progression
	1. Straight arm plank (3x45s, no hip sag)
	2. ==Planche lean (3x30s)==
	3. Frog stand (30s)
	4. Tuck planche (15s)
	5. Advanced tuck planche (15s, hips level & no pike)
	6. Straddle planche (12s)
	7. Full Planche
	
    Rest: 3 mins  
    Sets: 3
    Total time: ~8 mins

+ Planche Push-up Progression
	1. ==Push-ups (3x20)==
	2. Incline pseudo planche push-ups (3x12)
	3. Pseudo planche pushups (3x15)
	4. Lean pushups (3x12 reps)
	5. Tuck planche pushups (3x8 reps, controlled tempo)
	6. Advanced tuck pushups (3x6 reps)
	7. Straddle planche pushups
	   
    Rest: 3 mins  
    Sets: 3x5-10
    Total time: ~12 mins
    
- Front Lever Rows
    1. Inverted rows (3x12)
    2. ==Feet elevated inverted rows (3x10)==
    3. Tuck front lever rows (3x8, flat back & controlled pull to chest)
    4. Advanced tuck rows (3x8)
    5. Full front lever rows
    
    Rest: 3 mins  
    Sets: 3x5-8  
    Total time: ~8-9 mins
    
- Mobility/Compression Finisher
    1. Bodyweight Jefferson curls — 8 slow tempo (3-4s down, 3-4s up)
    2. Basic hamstring stretch — 45s/leg
    3. Pancake stretch — 90s, sinking deeper
    4. Pike compression holds — 3x20-30s (knees lifted, stop before form breaks)

## Sunday: Recovery

1. Aerobic primer (5-8 mins)
    - Could be walking, easy cycling, or swimming (not running)
2. Foam rolling / myofascial release (15 mins)
    - Calves — 90s/leg
    - Quads — 90s/leg
    - IT band — 60s/leg (light pressure)
    - Glutes/piriformis — 90s/side
    - Upper back/lats — 2 mins
    - Forearms — 60s/arm
3. Deep static stretching (18-20 mins)
    - Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 60-90s/side
    - Hamstring stretch — 60-90s/side
    - Pancake/straddle hold — 2 mins
    - Figure-4 glute stretch — 60s/side
    - Pigeon pose — 90s/side
    - Doorway pec stretch — 60s
    - Overhead lat stretch — 60s/side
4. Joint mobility / CARS circuit (12-15 mins)
    - Shoulder CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per arm
    - HIP CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per leg
    - Ankle CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per foot
    - Wrist CARs — 5 slow reps per direction per hand
    - Cat-cow into thread-the-needle — 8 reps then 6 rotations, per side
5. Parasympathetic down-regulation (5 mins)
    - Box breathing (4s in, 4s hold, 6s out) — 5 mins total
        - Laying down, legs elevated against wall

Total time: ~57-63mins
