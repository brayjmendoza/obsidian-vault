### Ingredients
- ¾ cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 scoop whey protein powder 
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup unsweetened oat milk
- ¾ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1–2 tbsp chopped walnuts
- ~1 cup mixed berries, fresh or frozen

### Recipe
1. In a 32oz mason jar, add in order:
	1. The Foundation (oats & seeds) — oats, chia seeds, flaxseeds, honey, and vanilla extract
	2. The Flavor Boosters (powders & spices) — protein powder, cinnamon, salt
	3. The Core (sweeteners & thick add-ins) — Greek yogurt, mixed berries 
	4. The Catalyst (liquids) — oat milk
2. Stir very thoroughly with spoon
3. Shake vigorously for 10-15 seconds
4. Let sit for 5 minutes
5. Give one final quick stir
6. Refrigerate overnight, ideally 6–12 hours.
7. In the morning, optionally add toppings:
	1. A splash of oat milk if too thick
	2. Crunchy toppings — walnuts, granola, coconut chips, etc
	3. Fresh fruit — sliced bananas, diced applies, fresh berries
	4. Final drizzle — honey or dollop of peanut butter
