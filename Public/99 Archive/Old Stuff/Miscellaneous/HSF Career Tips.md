## Interview Tactic: FEB
+ F - Feature
	+ Talk about a feature about yourself
		+ Innovative, conscientious, diligent, hard-working, leadership, communication, etc.
+ E - Example
	+ A personal experience/example that illustrates the feature
		+ Coming up with the SQL module idea for Prof. Dodds (Scripting For All) shows that I'm innovative
+ B - Benefit
	+ How does the feature benefit the employer?

## ASK to GET
+ A - Actively Commit
+ S - Skillfully Articulate Your Value
+ K - Keep a winning resume
+ G - Get ready to interview like a pro
+ E - End with great questions and a strong close
	+ Asking questions during the interview
		+ What does it take to be successful at this company?
		+ What does your best employee do differently than everybody else?
		+ What does your management training program look like?
		+ Do you have any concerns about my background being a good fit for this position that I can address?
	+ Closing the interview
		+ Know what's the next step
		+ Know where I stand
		+ Grab a business card
		+ Send a thank you letter
+ T - Target your dream companies
