Types of investments
+ Cash (lower risk, lower returns)
+ Bond
+ Stocks (higher risk, higher returns)

## Stocks
---
Stocks also known as equities, give stockholders a share of ownership in a company (so you literally own a (small) percentage of the company)
+ Benefits: Share price can increase, some stocks can pay dividends, ability to **vote shares?**
+ Risks: share price can decrease, companies can go bankrupt

## Bonds
---
Bonds are like loans you make to a government or corporation on which you receive interest payments for a period of time
+ Benefits: predictable income stream, return of principal after bond matures
+ Risks: inflation risk, issuer may "call" the bond, issuers may go bankrupt
+ You buy bonds, interest rates are usually fixed
	+ So if interest rates go up after buying a bond, that's bad
	+ If interest rates go down after buying a bond, that's good

## Mutual Funds & ETFs
---
+ Mutual funds are collections of stocks and bonds that are "bundled together"
	+ Price goes with natural growth of the market
