# Folder Structure

Inside `Public/`, a numbered PARA-style layout separates finite projects, ongoing hobbies, life admin, and knowledge. The first digit is the band (the kind of folder); the second is the position within the band. Subfolders are not numbered. `structure-schema.yaml` is the authority on which folders may exist (see [[Schemas and Validation]]). As an example:

```
Public/
├── 00 Inbox/            quick captures; Claude triages
├── 01 Logs/             Daily/, Weekly/, Monthly/ (generated by skills)
├── 10 Projects/         finite goals with an end date
├── 20 Hobbies/          one domain per hobby (see Domain Skeleton)
│   ├── Music/           Composers/, Piano/, Violin/ (instruments are subdomains)
│   ├── Fitness/
│   └── Travel/
├── 30 Life Admin/       one domain per responsibility (see Domain Skeleton)
│   ├── Finance/
│   ├── Health/
│   ├── Home/
│   ├── Planning/
│   └── Work/
├── 40 Knowledge/
│   ├── Sources/         Papers/, Books/, Courses/, Articles/
│   ├── Concepts/        atomic notes, one idea each (flat)
│   └── Maps/            one hub per field: CS, Math, Physics, Music Theory, Philosophy
├── 50 References/       look-up info and how-tos; at most one level of topic 
subfolders
|   └── Bookmarks/       links to interesting online tools and visualizations
├── 90 System/           Templates/, Bases/, Claude/ (CONVENTIONS.md, changelog), Overview/ (these notes)
├── 91 Tooling/          schemas, validation scripts, tests
├── 98 Attachments/      images, PDFs, and files embedded in notes
└── 99 Archive/          retired notes; mirrors the original path
```

The folders under `20 Hobbies/` and `30 Life Admin/` are domains, and they all share one shape: see [[Domain Skeleton]]. Which note types each folder holds is listed in [[Note Types and Properties]].

## Bands

The first digit of a top-level folder is its band (i.e., the kind of folder it is). The second digit is its position within the band. Numbers only set sort order; subfolders are not numbered. The binding version of this table is in [[CONVENTIONS]] (section 2).

| Band | Kind             | Folders                                                       | Holds                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0    | Capture and logs | `00 Inbox/`, `01 Logs/`                                       | Material ordered by time, not filed by topic: unsorted captures waiting for triage, and generated daily, weekly, and monthly logs    |
| 1    | Projects         | `10 Projects/`                                                | Goals with a finish line                                                                                                            |
| 2    | Hobbies          | `20 Hobbies/`                                                 | Hobbies you keep doing, one domain each (see [[Domain Skeleton]])                                                                   |
| 3    | Life Admin       | `30 Life Admin/`                                              | Responsibilities that never end, one domain each (see [[Domain Skeleton]])                                                          |
| 4    | Knowledge        | `40 Knowledge/`                                               | Things learned: sources, concepts, maps                                                                                             |
| 5    | References       | `50 References/`                                              | Things looked up: how-tos and look-up info                                                                                          |
| 6–8  | Unused           | none                                                          | Reserved for new kinds of content that fit none of bands 1–5                                                                        |
| 9    | System           | `90 System/`, `91 Tooling/`, `98 Attachments/`, `99 Archive/` | Not content. The vault's rules, docs, and tooling count up from 90; storage for embedded files and retired notes counts down from 99 |

- **Content lives in bands 1–5,** in the same order as the [[#Placement Rules]] questions.
- **New top-level folders** take the band matching their kind and the next free position in it (a second capture folder would be `02 …`). Like any new folder, one needs your approval and a schema edit first (see [[Claude Guardrails]]).
- **Bands 6–8 are empty on purpose:** room for a new kind of content without renumbering anything.

## Placement Rules

Ask in order:

1. Has a finish line → `10 Projects/`
2. A hobby you keep doing → `20 Hobbies/`
3. A responsibility that never ends → `30 Life Admin/`
4. Something learned → `40 Knowledge/`
5. Something looked up → `50 References/`

Nothing fits clearly → it stays in `00 Inbox/`. For Claude-generated notes that end up here, Claude will ask a tagged question on where to place it (see [[Claude Guardrails]]).

## Knowledge is Organized by Links

`40 Knowledge/` is the one content band not organized by topic folders. Its folders sort notes by kind. These "kinds" are sources (where you learned something), concepts (one idea each, in a single flat folder), and maps (hand-curated tables of contents, one per field). Subjects come from internal links. A concept like the Fourier transform sits once in `Concepts/` and is linked from the Math, Physics, and Music Theory maps. See [[Knowledge Structure]] for the full picture.
