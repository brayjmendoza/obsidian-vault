# Vault Rollout

Five phases. The test with real content in phase 2 matters most, because schemas change once real notes hit them (see [[Vault Principles|schemas before automation]]).

1. **Skeleton:** set up the vault and its Public/Private split (see [[Privacy Boundary]]), the domains (see [[Domain Skeleton]]), the conventions and schemas with their validation and CI (see [[Schemas and Validation]]), templates for every note type, and Git sync (see [[Sync and Backup]]).
2. **Real content:** migrate repertoire, the current routines, a few papers, and a past trip. Adjust the schemas.
3. **First skills:** daily-note and inbox-triage, used for two weeks (see [[Claude Skills]]).
4. **Core skills:** recall, weekly-review, and ingest-source.
5. **Automation:** hobby planners, spending-import, quiz-me, and vault-health on a schedule.
